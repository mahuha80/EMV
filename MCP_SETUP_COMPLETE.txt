💻 USAGE EXAMPLES

Python:
  import json, subprocess
  p = subprocess.Popen(['python3', 'random_even_mcp.py'],
                       stdin=subprocess.PIPE,
                       stdout=subprocess.PIPE, text=True)
  req = {"jsonrpc": "2.0", "method": "get_random_even", "params": {}, "id": 1}
  p.stdin.write(json.dumps(req) + "\n")
  p.stdin.flush()
  response = json.loads(p.stdout.readline())
  print(response)

Bash:
  echo '{"jsonrpc": "2.0", "method": "get_all_evens", "params": {}, "id": 1}' | \
    python3 random_even_mcp_package/random_even_mcp.py
